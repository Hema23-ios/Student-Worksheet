Grade 7: Microsoft Excel Charts

Part 1: Explore Excel Chart Types

Examine each chart diagram and read its uses to understand when to apply them in Excel.

1. Column Chart

Best For: Comparing values across discrete categories using vertical bars.

Scenario: Comparing the total number of students in Grade 6, Grade 7, and Grade 8.

2. Bar Chart

Best For: Comparing categories when names are long or text-heavy using horizontal bars.

Scenario: Comparing election votes for candidates with long names.

3. Line Chart

Best For: Displaying continuous trends or changes over continuous time periods.

Scenario: Tracking temperature changes every hour throughout a full day.

4. Pie Chart

Best For: Displaying breakdown proportions or percentages of a whole total (100%).

Scenario: Showing how your daily pocket money is split into savings, food, and games.

5. Area Chart

Best For: Emphasizing the volume or total magnitude of change over time.

Scenario: Showing total water consumption of a city month-by-month over a year.

6. Scatter (X Y) Chart

Best For: Showing relationship patterns or correlations between two numerical variables.

Scenario: Plotting height (cm) versus weight (kg) to see if taller students weigh more.
